Privacy Policy

Nami Finances · Effective October 15, 2026

Nami Finances is a personal, single-user application. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app uses read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It only reads bank purchase notification emails, and from them only extracts the purchase amount, currency, merchant name, date and the last four digits of the card. It never sends, modifies or deletes email.

How data is used and stored

Extracted purchases are stored in the owner's own Google Cloud project, used solely to display a personal spending dashboard, and are not shared, sold or used for advertising. To suggest a spending category, merchant names and amounts may be sent to Google's Vertex AI (Gemini) service within that same project; no names, email addresses or card numbers are sent.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted by deleting the app's Google Cloud project data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in consent screen.
